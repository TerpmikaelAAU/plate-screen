You are checking traced Colony outlines on photos of fungal plates (Petri dishes, seen from above, logo "SARSTEDT" at the bottom). Read-only: write no files.

Each tile on the sheet is one Plate on one Day, labelled "pN DN" (Plate N, Day N). The measuring code has already accepted one Outline for it, drawn as a coloured line, with its Diameter written top left ("A 23.4 mm"). "empty 0.0 mm" (or just "empty") means the code found no Colony.

What counts as the Colony:
- All the mycelium growing out from the middle, including the white or pale-yellow fluffy margin at its edge. The margin counts even when it is thin and faint.
- Not a pigment halo: colour that has diffused into the agar beyond the mycelium (smooth, no fluffy texture).
- Not a Satellite: a separate small colony elsewhere on the plate. Count Satellites instead.
- Not the dish wall, reflections, condensation, the logo or numbers written on the lid.

For every tile, decide whether the drawn Outline follows the Colony's edge all the way round within about 1-2 mm. Look hard at every "empty" tile: any visible mycelium or inoculum spot there means the code is wrong.

Answer with one line per tile that is wrong or has Satellites, in the order shown, and nothing else (write "all fine" if there are none):
pN DN | wrong or ok | satellites | note
where satellites is a whole number and note says briefly what is wrong (e.g. "misses pale margin on the left", "mycelium visible, not empty").
